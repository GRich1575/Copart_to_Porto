Vehicle Import & Resale Intelligence Platform
I recommend building this as a modular Python application, with PostgreSQL as the central database, Streamlit as the business dashboard, and independent data collectors for auctions, local vehicle markets, regulations, logistics and repair costs.

The key architectural decision is to make this a vehicle sourcing and import profitability platform, rather than simply a web scraper.

The system should ultimately answer five business questions:

1. Which vehicles should we buy?
    - Honda Gold Wing 1800 (GL1800 F6B or something like that) no earlier that 2018 year of edition, mileage <= 50000 miles
2. Where should we buy them, and at what maximum auction price?
    - Hasn't determined yet. Analyze the history of closed auctions for similar vehicles. 
3. What will the complete import, repair and registration process cost?
    - Complete import from USA to Europe ~ $350 for container + document fees ~$100
4. What is the realistic resale price in the destination country?
    - Less than price of the same used vehicles at least for 10%
5. What is the expected profit, investment return and risk?
    - Minimal edge is 1500-2000 euros/dollars per vehicle.

2. Business acquisition strategy
The platform should analyze completed auctions, not just current listings.

For every historical motorcycle, capture:
- Final hammer price, where available.
- Auction fees.
- Model year and version.
- Mileage.
- Damage category.
- Title status.
- Auction location.
- Sale date.
- Whether the vehicle actually sold.
- Repairability and parts availability.
Then calculate price distributions for comparable motorcycles.

The initial acquisition strategy should use three categories:
1. Clean-title motorcycles with minor damage.
2. Salvage-title motorcycles with repairable cosmetic or mechanical damage.
3. Motorcycles with significant structural damage, which should initially be excluded.
Structural damage to the frame, steering or suspension should trigger a high-risk flag rather than being treated as an ordinary repair estimate.

5. Critical Portugal import and registration risk
One issue deserves priority before buying anything: a US-market motorcycle may not have the European Certificate of Conformity (CoC) or European type approval.

The Portuguese IMT provides different registration procedures depending on the motorcycle's documentation and homologation status. Its published fees include €45 for one used-motorcycle procedure with a CoC and €165 for a procedure without national homologation, subject to the applicable conditions. These are not necessarily the total costs of legalization. 
Instituto da Mobilidade e dos Transportes+1


I would make homologation eligibility a mandatory pre-purchase check, not something to investigate after the motorcycle arrives.
